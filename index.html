What is Poppo Live?+

Poppo Live is a live-streaming and social platform operated by VSHOW PTE. LTD. It includes live broadcasts, multi-user rooms, short-form posts, messaging, profiles, and virtual gifts.

How do I become a Poppo host?+

Create a secure account, find your Poppo ID, review the rules, complete required verification, and follow the current creator or agency onboarding flow. Approval is not guaranteed.

What is a Poppo agency?+

A Poppo agency is an independent partner that recruits, onboards, supports, and manages creators under the platform's current program terms.

Does Poppo guarantee income?+

No. Creator and agency earnings vary with eligible gifts, activity, engagement, performance, program rules, and compliance. No fixed amount is guaranteed.

Where should I download Poppo Live?+

Use the official Poppo website, Google Play, or Apple App Store. Avoid unknown APK mirrors or desktop installers.

How do I contact support?+

For our agency onboarding, WhatsApp +91 78383 17307. For account, moderation, recharge, or payout issues, use Poppo's in-app support or official contact details.
