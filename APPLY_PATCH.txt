IMPORTANT: Keep your existing translations/ folder. It contains the completed translations.

1. Copy these 3 .py files into your existing site_work folder and choose Replace:
   - extract_translations.py
   - build_locales.py
   - validate_locales.py

2. Activate your existing virtual environment:
   .venv\Scripts\activate

3. Rebuild WITHOUT translating again:
   python prepare_multilingual.py --skip-translate

The build will remove/recreate each locale folder, so accidental locale paths such as
ur\.venv\Lib\site-packages\torch\utils\model_dump\skeleton.html
will be removed automatically.

Do NOT delete translations/*.json.
